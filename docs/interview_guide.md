# Interview Guide

## Phase 00 Questions

1. What is point-in-time integrity, and why does it matter in trading research?
2. Why should exchange timestamp and local receipt timestamp both be stored?
3. What is look-ahead bias?
4. Why is a schema layer useful before building collectors?
5. What is a crossed book, and why should it be rejected?
6. Why should a research model be frozen before optimizing trading rules?
7. Why should failed experiments be logged?
8. How would you explain the difference between fair-value estimation and execution
   simulation?

## Expected Themes

Good answers should emphasize causality, observability, timestamp ordering, data quality,
and the difference between statistically interesting signals and executable trading edge.
